Jinghan Yang

My research focuses on both the performance and robustness of computer vision systems, with an emphasis on autonomous driving. Recently, I have concentrated on designing techniques to improve the effectiveness of video panoptic segmentation models on large-scale autonomous driving datasets. Throughout my previous research, I studied the vulnerabilities and limitations of deep learning models in vision and autonomous driving, with a focus on developing methods that remain reliable under real-world conditions.
